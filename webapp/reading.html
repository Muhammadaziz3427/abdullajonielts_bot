READING PASSAGE 1: The Secrets of Sleep

Sleep is a vital, often neglected, component of every human's overall health and well-being. Sleep is important because it enables the body to repair and be fit and ready for another day. Getting adequate rest may also help prevent excess weight gain, heart disease, and increased illness duration.

During sleep, your brain cycles through two types of sleep: rapid eye movement (REM) sleep and non-REM sleep. Non-REM sleep is essential for bone and muscle growth, tissue repair, and immune system replenishment. REM sleep plays an important role in memory consolidation and mood regulation.
